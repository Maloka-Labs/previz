Consent & Privacy Policy

Part 1: Mini FAQ

What happens after hitting the VYBE MY TWIN button?

Our automated system will scan up to 100 of your last YouTube videos, where available, and up to 100 of your last public Instagram posts (nothing private, no DMs, no login required), to automatically generate your prototype AI twin — what we call a v0.5 'ProtoTwin' — for your private review.

What happens after my prototype twin is vybed?

You guys get to hang out together for a 14-day private review period, at the end of which you will be invited to move on to stage 2: the creation of your Good Vybe Guru twin, requiring an additional training-run via a Q&A series of voice-notes about your work as a practitioner (around a dozen questions for your reply, whenever suits your schedule). Importantly, you can choose to delete your ProtoTwin at any time during the 14-day review period with a single click, no harm no foul.

Where would my final Guru twin hang out?

Starting on Telegram, your final Good Vybe Guru will only hang out in public with your prior approval, and can be retired at any stage — you are always in control.

What happens to my data?

Your data is encrypted and stored securely, we will never share it with third parties. Like your voice-notes, the twin data remains yours and can be deleted at any stage.

By tapping VYBE MY TWIN, you authorise Vybable Ltd., a subsidiary of Maloka Labs Ltd., to collect and process your public Instagram and YouTube content for the purpose described above, in accordance with our Privacy Policy (below).
